# Academic References and KM Foundations

This document lists the scholarly sources that inform the Knowledge Management (KM) strategy for the **Pixel Pushers** project. These references provide the theoretical basis for our application's design, specifically regarding Wiig’s Knowledge Management Cycle.

## 1. Core Framework Source
**Wiig, K. M. (1993). *Knowledge Management Foundations: Thinking about Thinking - How People and Organizations Create, Represent, and Use Knowledge*. Schema Press.**
* **KM Application:** This is the primary source for our chosen framework. It defines the four phases (Building, Holding, Pooling, and Applying) that govern how our application captures and retrieves student project data.

## 2. Knowledge Conversion Theory
**Nonaka, I., & Takeuchi, H. (1995). *The Knowledge-Creating Company*. Oxford University Press.**
* **KM Application:** This source provides the foundation for our "Upload Form" design. It explains how "Tacit" knowledge (the unwritten experience of students) is converted into "Explicit" knowledge (structured data in our database) through the process of Externalization.

## 3. Repository and Pooling Strategy
**Davenport, T. H., & Prusak, L. (1998). *Working Knowledge: How Organizations Manage What They Know*. Harvard Business Press.**
* **KM Application:** Used to justify the "Pooling" phase of our project. This text emphasizes the value of knowledge repositories and standardized taxonomies, which we implemented through our tagging system and searchable archive.
